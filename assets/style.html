Executive Summary

The Problem

Service requirements are often managed through calls and chats. Leads can be missed, technician availability is uncertain, customer updates are inconsistent, and completion/payment proof may be weak.

The Solution

ORACLIME creates a structured workflow from lead generation and technician assignment to service execution, completion verification, payment recording and closure.

The Opportunity

Start with focused technical services, prove operational reliability and unit economics, then expand partners, technicians, categories and geography.

06

Technician Workflow

NEW → ASSIGNED → ACCEPTED → EN_ROUTE → ARRIVED → IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETION_SUBMITTED → COMPLETED

Exceptions: REJECTED • RESCHEDULE_REQUESTED • CUSTOMER_UNAVAILABLE • CANCELLED • CORRECTION_REQUIRED • PAYMENT_PENDING

Accept / Reject

Structured rejection reasons are mandatory.

Reschedule / Unavailable

Previous schedules stay in history. Customer unavailable never counts as completion.

Correction

Completed submissions lock unless admin opens specific fields for correction.

07

Verification & Payment

Customer OTP

Expiry, attempt limit, resend control and verification timestamp.

Evidence

Indoor location, indoor serial, outdoor location, outdoor serial and AC bill photos.

Payment

Service amount, discount, final amount, collected amount, outstanding, status, mode and reference.

Outstanding = Final Amount − Collected Amount
